The vulva has three openings: the urethra, the vagina and the anus. Most of the clitoris is internal.

The urinary tract

Front to back: the urethral opening (urine), the vaginal opening (menstruation, sex, birth) and the anus. The female urethra is about 4 cm long, against about 20 cm in men, which is why UTIs are so common.
