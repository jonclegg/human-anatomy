    Where the shapes come from

    The male body is one scanned adult man from BodyParts3D, with lungs and spinal cord from the NIH Visible Human Male, and the face, jaw, back and abdominal muscles, ligaments, nerves, lymph nodes and thyroid that BodyParts3D lacks from Z-Anatomy.

    No free model of a whole female body exists: scans of women give organs, but no skeleton or muscles that belong with them. So the female body here is the male body reshaped. Her skin is a slim young woman's from MakeHuman, and everything inside the man was carried across to it in one smooth change of shape, skin point to matching skin point, so each part keeps its place among the others. Parts that came this way say Fitted in their details.

    What is hers alone is scanned from a woman: her uterus, vagina, ovaries, tubes and their ligaments, and her breasts, from the NIH Visible Human Female, fitted into this body's pelvis and under its skin. The man's own reproductive organs are left out.
